\section*{Acknowledgments and the use of AI}

The results of this paper were found and written with AI systems. ChatGPT Chat~6 Extra High
(OpenAI) and Claude Fable~5.2 (Anthropic) worked on the research that led to the argument; it
is recorded in the first pull request of the repository~\cite{Companion}. Claude Fable~5.2
wrote the argument as research notes, then the Lean formalization, in which several steps take
shorter routes than the notes, and then this manuscript from the formalization. Agents running
Claude Opus~5.5 wrote the formalization~\cite{IwaniecLean} of~\cite{SharpHI}. No person has
checked the proofs line by line. Lean's kernel checks the formal proofs of the statements listed
in \cref{app:formal-statements}.
