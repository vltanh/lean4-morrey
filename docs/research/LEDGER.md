## Current decision

Unrestricted planar Morrey remains open. Keep P01–P03 as verified assets. Archive the specialty ladders rather than deleting results. R11–R15 now give a fixed complete normalized class, fold-safe and integer-fill mechanisms, and an explicit finite-accuracy cutoff on BOTH integrand and geometry. The principal open question is exact zero, not existence of a positive-accuracy cutoff. Seek a uniform gap amplification or a genuine universal positive mechanism; the enormous finite bounds are theoretical and have not been solved. Do not resume a polynomial-degree or vertex-count ladder. Pursue a new universal construction or exact counterexample; only reopen a previously failed route under a changed, testable hypothesis. See [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
