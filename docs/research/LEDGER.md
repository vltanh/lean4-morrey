## Current decision

Unrestricted planar Morrey remains open. Keep P01–P03 as verified assets. Archive the specialty ladders rather than deleting results. The unrestricted compactification R11 and folding-safe extension R12 give a fixed compact complete energy class, a uniform finite-accuracy integrand-side reduction, an actual signed-trace operation across folds and a fixed compact stress target. Attack the remaining universal graph-law inequality, not a polynomial-degree ladder. Pursue a new universal construction or exact counterexample; only reopen a previously failed route under a changed, testable hypothesis. See [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
