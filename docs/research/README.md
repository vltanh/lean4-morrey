## Problem and reliable starting facts

For every finite-valued rank-one convex (RC) F on real 2×2 matrices, arbitrary base A and every periodic Lipschitz two-component map u, prove

    J_F(A,u) = integral [F(A+Du) - F(A)] >= 0.

A strict negative value with a globally RC F and an actual compatible gradient disproves the conjecture. Translation absorbs A, but need not preserve homogeneity. Finite RC implies local Lipschitz regularity; smooth approximation and periodic/affine-boundary bridges allow bounded test ranges for strict counterexamples. Rank-one convex quadratics are QC, and affine functions and the determinant are null Lagrangians.

Gradient laws obey the mean and determinant identities, but those identities alone do not establish gradient realizability or laminate status. Conditioning, Carathéodory reduction and abstract moment constraints need independent compatibility justification. Polyconvexity (PC) is not a necessary premise for this program. Pedregal's false universal nonemptiness premise refutes the proposed argument, not the conjecture.

### Unrestricted target

Let C be all globally C^{1,1} rank-one convex energies F on real 2×2 matrices with F(0)=DF(0)=0 and Lip DF≤1. For arbitrary bases A and periodic W^{1,2} two-component u of positive energy set

    E(u) = integral |Du|²
    J_F(A,u) = integral [F(A+Du)-F(A)]
    beta_* = inf_{F in C, A, u, E(u)>0} J_F(A,u)/E(u).

**Prove beta_*=0.** R16 records a hand reduction making this equivalent to the unrestricted finite-valued planar problem. P15/P16 record −1/8 < beta_* ≤ 0. Both need analytic/formal verification before final assembly. Do not assume an optimizer, polyconvexity, isotropy, homogeneity, bounded amplitudes, fixed support or fixed wave count.

### G1 — exact unrestricted proof (OPEN)

Choose a mechanism with a quantified implication valid for every normalized RC energy and every actual compatible gradient. The following are sufficient alternatives, not additional assumptions of Morrey:

1. **Self-contraction:** prove beta_* ≥ theta beta_* for an explicit 0≤theta<1. Since beta_*≤0, this forces equality. A computer-assisted proof needs a sound universal reduction and an exact LP/SOS/interval/algebraic certificate, not merely a finite collection of necessary tests.
2. **Gap separation:** prove beta_*=0 or beta_*≤−epsilon for an explicit epsilon>0, then certify beta_*>−epsilon using a justified finite computation or an analytic bound. R16 supplies positive-accuracy enclosures, not the separation.
3. **Direct rigidity:** for b=−beta_*>0, H=F+b|.|² is QC and strongly RC, not necessarily PC. Uniformly exclude vanishing relative stabilized residual at fixed b (for example, a valid j≥c b^N with positive c and finite N). Include all genuine near-extremizers, including concentration or vanishing absolute energy. A different direct global Jensen proof is equally acceptable.

**Current exact-zero focus (25 November).** P20–P26 identify b=-beta_* with the maximal martingale-dilation deficit, with attained common stress couplings, scalar subordination, uniform positive slack at every b>0, and exact cofactor transport. P27 adds simultaneous covariance domination in each row or column metric family. The computational route R25–R28 now handles every splitting depth on a finite grid, gives a uniform positive-accuracy grid error, and maximizes over the entire continuous gradient mesh by rational dual vertices and weighted gradient projection, without enumerating nodal assignments. [Full derivations and limits](IWANIEC_MORREY_C11_OPTIMAL_MARGIN.md#11-25-november-simultaneous-directional-budgets-exact-whole-mesh-maximization-and-a-stop-rule-for-fixed-law-transforms).

**New stop rule N25.** All normalized first-order joint Hamiltonian tests at a fixed-law optimum already follow from its optimal joint laminate and conditional residual. The flat face cannot extend beyond its known endpoint if d>0; combining the flat segment with cofactor scaling and macro-preserving microscopic rearrangements cannot strictly amplify the universal spatial ratio, even with perfect capture of the residual. Do not keep tuning those parameters or treat P25 integrated slack as an extension of the flat face.

**Next genuine gate.** Establish a compatibility-changing operation for the original spatial fields that produces a strictly larger ratio than a=1+2b at hypothetical b>0, or prove a uniform exact geometry cutoff or effective separation of b from zero. P19's fixed-energy stress rigidity and R20–R24's compatible critical-state reduction remain possible tools, but their constants cannot be transferred to arbitrary varying energies without a new argument. R28 is only a positive-accuracy certificate; its target-grid artifact is explicitly nonzero even on an entire family of true laminates. No strict contraction, improved numerical universal bound, or exact-zero closure has been obtained.

**Exit:** prove an unrestricted exact-zero inequality, or supply a sound uniform finite exhaustive reduction and certify all its cases. A new negative bound or necessary condition alone is not gate closure.

### G2 — exact counterexample alternative (OPEN)

The [global certificate hierarchy](GLOBAL_CERTIFICATES.md) is complete for strict negative witnesses but does not terminate in the positive case. Search only where a new structural prediction makes a discriminating test worthwhile.

- Verify the corrected base-minimizer range/kernel condition and known planar-positive and higher-dimensional-negative controls.
- Save a single globally RC energy (exact LH/Gram or other global certificate), a compatible rational periodic PL field or equivalent affine-boundary test, and a rigorously negative integral with all errors included. Save input data, code, settings, repair and exact rejection reason.
- Never interpret a non-PC energy, a sampled LH pass, a failed laminate construction or finite non-detection as a counterexample or as proof of the conjecture.

**Exit:** one certified genuine planar negative pair.

### Stop rules for the unrestricted route

Before any substantial computation, state the exact global implication, why every unrestricted candidate is covered, and the smallest test that could refute the proposed mechanism. No atom-, frequency-, mesh-, degree-, exponent- or subclass ladder without a proved stopping bound. A finite parameterization or arbitrarily accurate finite approximation is not an exact decision procedure. Record negative approaches once in the ledger; reopen only with a changed, testable hypothesis.

Unrestricted closure means a proof of beta_*=0, a justified finite exact proof with every case certified, or a certified negative planar witness. The separately requested structural extension P28 is not such a closure.
