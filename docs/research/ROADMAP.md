## G1 — genuinely global proof route (open)

Seek a construction or theorem acting on **every** bounded compatible planar gradient law. Do not start by adding another exponent, rotation group, wave count, support cardinality, or separated profile.

Before pursuing a route, write the following:

- exact universal hypothesis and exact implication to RC Jensen;
- what new spatial compatibility information it uses (not just determinant, covariance, connectedness, or fixed known tests);
- a single operation with quantified progress, or a finite exhaustive reduction with a justified size bound;
- the first falsification test from [LEDGER](LEDGER.md#negative-results-and-invalid-inferences), and why the construction is not already refuted.

**New post-reset global reduction (R11):** [parametric compactification](IWANIEC_MORREY_PARAMETRIC_COMPACTIFICATION.md) removes both homogeneity and isotropy restrictions by an exact graph-bivector extension. The entire integrand cone lives on a fixed compact S²×S²; positive rotational averaging gives a uniform finite family of rational polynomial tests for any prescribed relative area error, and a compact positive curvature stress has mass exactly equal to the graph-area excess. Work on actual spatial compatibility in this geometry (slices, chart changes or another positive construction). Do not treat a finite-accuracy family as an exact finite cutoff. The [signed-trace folding theorem](IWANIEC_MORREY_SIGNED_TRACE_FOLD_TRANSFER.md) now constructs an actual compatible gradient across arbitrary degree-one folded projections (W¹,¹ under a.e. nonsingularity, with analytic approximation for general maps). Its explicit penalty makes the canonical extension QC iff the chart energy is QC, and the RC/QC envelopes commute. This settles folding for that extension class and closes both E and E2 benchmarks. The entire unrestricted problem reduces exactly to W between |X|−1 and |X|, equal to |X|−1 outside radius 3/2, at zero base with |Dphi|≤1/2. The remaining universal step is QC of this fixed compact RC class or a positive curvature stress. Do not assume an arbitrary existing parametric integrand satisfies the fold budget, or that the new all-chart QC generators form a complete cone.

**New law-side mechanism (R13):** [Integer fillings and BV trace](IWANIEC_MORREY_INTEGER_FILLINGS.md) removes regular projection and parametrized-disk assumptions. For the fixed normalized class, the graph cell infimum equals that over integer polyhedral fillings confined over the square; a strict negative filling without confinement also gives an actual negative smooth gradient after BV trace smoothing. The integer linear objective with exact incidence equations is a new complete search route. At any prescribed negative-gap tolerance the added area penalty gives a uniform mass and weighted fold budget. Seek a universal integer-valid inequality or boundary simplification, or a certified negative filling; do not relax to real coefficients (the verified-L control has gap at least 1/10 between integer and relaxed optima). No finite exact vertex cutoff is proved. The exact verifier and finite MILP discovery prototype are implemented and tested on controls.

**New finite-level interface (R14):** [Positive fillings and degree-free finite optimization](IWANIEC_MORREY_DEGREE_FREE_FINITE_FILLINGS.md) proves that positive projection integer fills are binary Lipschitz graphs, with at most 2N−6 triangles for N projected vertices. A finite rational LP/MILP over all Lipschitz and rank-one midpoint data has an explicit global RC repair, giving a degree-free two-sided error ≤epsilon/2 on the entire normalized integrand cone for any fixed rational vertex set. The conservative complete net is not yet practical. Executed joint polynomial searches, exact lower-bound replay and exact geometry enumeration provide finite controls (32 and 1142 fillings certified for all RC F). Do not treat these controls as an unrestricted solution. The integrand approximation has an explicit stopping criterion at any positive accuracy; R15 below now gives a geometry cutoff at positive accuracy as well.

**New unrestricted quantitative reduction (R15):** [Uniform geometric cutoff](IWANIEC_MORREY_UNIFORM_GEOMETRY_CUTOFF.md) proves a computable mesh and rational nodal grid for every prescribed positive normalized gap epsilon. Separated Fourier bands, sparse discarded scales, actual localized compatible potentials, and L²-relative interpolation control the total error independently of the original oscillation complexity. With R14, a finite rational optimization at that tolerance either yields a genuine negative pair or proves J>−epsilon uniformly over the normalized unrestricted class. For a fixed smooth energy with uniform second/third derivative bounds, the same argument compares the global relative Jensen margin on bounded gradients to the finite-mesh margin with arbitrary prescribed relative error; a certified positive mesh margin gives exact positivity on that bounded range. These are theoretical finite reductions: even the conservative mesh side for epsilon=1/10 is 2^19456360. No such enormous optimization was executed. **Remaining exact gate:** a uniform positive lower bound on the magnitude of a possible normalized violation, a gap-amplifying transformation, or a new all-field zero-defect proof. Positive-accuracy closure is not exact Morrey closure. Do not spend the next session merely increasing the mesh.

**Exact-zero continuation (R16):** [Global C¹,¹ relative localization](IWANIEC_MORREY_C11_OPTIMAL_MARGIN.md) extends relative localization to arbitrary W¹,² amplitudes and requires only Lip DF. Hessian-normalization-preserving finite-data repair gives rational enclosures of the universal optimal relative margin beta_* itself. For a fixed known positive LH margin m, a finite mesh yields either exact strong QC or a genuine negative witness for the softened, still strongly RC energy. This is a finite alternative at known condition number, not an unrestricted exact decision. Matrix rescaling preserves the normalized deficit, while quadratic addition shifts both margins and can erase the violation. There is no justified fixed condition-ratio reduction. The remaining target is beta_*=0 or an effective separation from zero, not another tolerance/mesh ladder.

**Exact symmetry and critical-state gate (R17):** [Cofactor shear duality and universal margin bound](IWANIEC_MORREY_COFACTOR_DUALITY.md) proves a nonlinear cofactor transform preserves both RC and QC and commutes with their finite envelopes. If beta_*<0, there are normalized near-equality sequences of uniformly strongly RC QC energies, with genuine gradient and asymptotically compatible cofactor stress; all safe shears transport these sequences. Attack their **energy rigidity**, not stationarity alone (nontrivial stationary solutions to strongly QC systems are known). The same note proves beta_*≥−99/200 by a new anisotropic use of the old determinant transport bound; this is not an iterating contraction. The sup envelope gap is shear-invariant; a uniform normalized gap amplifier is still missing. No accuracy-independent exact cutoff or unrestricted resolution is claimed.

**Higher-dimensional Hamiltonian continuation (R18):** [Joint 4×2 Hamiltonian flow](IWANIEC_MORREY_JOINT_HAMILTONIAN_FLOW.md) proves preservation of planar RC under any globally C¹,¹ 4×2 RC Hamiltonian and forward QC under QC Hamiltonians. Its explicit second-order jet bound transfers a sufficiently negative joint gradient/cofactor-stress gap to a genuine planar counterexample. At an exact QC equality state that joint field is compatible in 4×2; the verified higher-dimensional counterexamples are possible Hamiltonians, but their witnesses are not automatically constitutive lifts. At equality of a planar laminate, the joint law is itself a 4×2 laminate. The exact finite polynomial/SOS jet verifier and regression controls are implemented. The new attack is to realize a qualifying negative joint gap or use the necessary critical-state inequality to prove rigidity. No qualifying negative joint field, amplifier, or universal proof has been established.

**Full stationary lift continuation (R19):** [Full stationary lift and local repair](IWANIEC_MORREY_FULL_STATIONARY_LIFT.md) adds the energy-momentum conservation law, producing a genuine 6×2 compatible field at QC equality. The null LH tangent identities permit a local explicit O(t²) curvature repair and exact global RC extension, without integrating a Hamiltonian flow or requiring global Hessian bounds on the input. A small QC gap controls inner-stress stationarity, with L² compatibility approximation on uniformly bounded gradients. Direct full-rank linear embeddings of the classical Sverak field and the canonical Cassese power-block assignment cannot supply an exact constitutive lift; nonlinear projections and other row remixings remain open. Seek a qualifying negative joint gap or universal energy rigidity, and control critical-state concentration rather than resuming mesh or exponent ladders. No unrestricted solution.

**Current active gate (R20):** [Quadratic-tail critical states and exact clipping](IWANIEC_MORREY_QUADRATIC_TAIL_CRITICAL.md) removes high-amplitude concentration for the critical-state route. Any strict unrestricted counterexample produces ONE fixed smooth globally QC, uniformly strongly RC H with an exact quadratic exterior. The verified scalar projection lemma, applied rowwise, gives energy-improving compatible clipping at a fixed radius and preserves the vanishing relative Jensen gap. The inner-stress error is O(delta^(1/4) sqrt(E)), and a second application of the scalar projection bounds the entire compatible six-row lift. A fixed compact annulus retains at least m/(4M) of relative variance. Absolute E may still tend to zero; the estimates explicitly allow that and do not rescale gradients by 1/sqrt(E). The new relative second-variation inequality implies a common 12×12 PSD stability condition on the compact weighted critical state. **Next goal:** exclude these bounded critical states by using their energy near equality, second variation and actual spatial compatibility; do not merely classify more stationary configurations or increase mesh size. The high-amplitude obstruction to the full lift is closed, the central rigidity theorem is not.

Two earlier mathematical interfaces remain available if a new idea fits:

**Gradient-law recursion:** D=0 iff laminate. An input-adaptive coarse law B resolving a fixed positive fraction of variance, with arbitrarily small determinant defect and compatible-child approximation error, implies a global contraction. The terminal minor-only trap geometries are already classified and incompatible as original gradient laws. The uniform partial-resolution criterion is equivalent in the existential sense to Morrey: proving it demands genuinely new spatial preservation. The old greedy split and Delta-only versions are refuted (N09–N10). Do not treat another detector or equivalent norm as closing G1.

**Positive stress/duality:** for every compatible law construct a nonnegative rank-one Hessian measure giving its Jensen functional on all smooth tests. The earlier compactness proof permits finite-degree certificates at all degrees but does not establish feasibility. Heat gives the correct PSD source, integration by parts yields signed cofactor cancellation; simultaneously preserving positivity and cancellation is the unresolved condition. Do not iterate a signed correction absent a contraction proof.

These are optional interfaces, not the only allowable strategies. A direct spatial inequality, contact-set construction for vector maps, or different global mechanism is welcome if it meets the quantified acceptance criterion.

**Exit:** a rigorous universal all-gradient inequality, or an exact reduction with all its finitely many cases closed. If the proposed operation cannot preserve compatibility or make strict progress, record its failure and change the mechanism, not the dimension/degree.

## G2 — counterexample route in parallel (open)

Use the complete strict-negative certificate hierarchy, not the sampled angular LP as evidence. A valid result must consist of a single finite continuous integrand with global rank-one convexity, a genuine planar periodic Lipschitz gradient (or affine-boundary equivalent), a base, and a rigorously negative integral gap with every error included.

1. Repair any affected base optimization (range/kernel check) and verify a known exact negative higher-dimensional control and a planar positive control.
2. Design a focused search predicted by a new mechanism or a meaningful relaxation. Prefer exact witness-local and global curvature constraints, rational SOS/norm multipliers or a local-ball certificate plus global tail. No permanent fixed positive Gram floor.
3. Save all candidate data, code, seed, optimizer settings, budgets, repair and exact rejection reasons. Check the same integrand/witness independently. If the strict gap fails, log an attained result rather than a lower bound or proof of absence.
4. A certified negative pair ends the mathematical search. Without a justified cutoff, finite failure never decides the positive conjecture.

**Exit:** actual certified planar RC/non-QC pair, not non-PC, failed tree, failed support or sampled curvature. See [GLOBAL_CERTIFICATES](GLOBAL_CERTIFICATES.md).
